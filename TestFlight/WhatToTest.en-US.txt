Explore keeps recipes saved for later separate from your everyday cookbook.

- Keep a recipe from What are you craving? and confirm it appears in Explore, beside Search, without appearing in Recipes.
- Move it to My Recipes, then back to Explore from the recipe menu. Edits, photos, reactions and other collection memberships should stay intact.
- Search should find recipes in both places. Adding a photo and title or importing a URL from Explore should save it there.
- Check the same separation on both household devices after sync. Existing recipes should stay in Recipes. Explore does not track cooking history.

Recipe discovery and chat:
- Discovery finds published recipes online using your configured OpenAI API key. Preview and edit a suggestion, swipe to keep or discard, undo a discard, and switch between stack and grid. Only kept recipes should save.
- Ask chat to adapt a dish from a published base. Review source links, servings, estimated seasonings and method changes before applying. Unsupported requests should ask a question or decline.
- Without a requested serving count, proposals should use the published recipe's servings and quantities. Check follow-ups, Preview, Apply, Undo and Save. Manual edits should remain protected.
- Preview photo changes against the original, apply them and undo. Check that cancelling leaves the saved recipe unchanged.

Regression checks:
- Search and filters after normal back, swipe-back and cancelled swipe-back.
- Adding, editing and reordering ingredients and sections; formatting review; tags and collections; full-screen method navigation; large text and Reduce Motion.
- Grocery scaling, merging, selection and clearing checked or unchecked items. Verify recipe and grocery sync between household devices.
- Household invitations, library switching and removal with two Apple accounts. Cancel must preserve everything; leaving another person's library must preserve their data. Check iCloud should show a result and retain useful error details.

CloudKit invitations and sharing require the production schema, including cloudkit.share. Verify these with signed builds and two Apple accounts.
