Supper has a warmer look, native Liquid Glass controls, and easier recipe browsing.

- Home: portrait and landscape photos stay square in the two-column grid. Check the title and search bar while searching and scrolling.
- Recipe: switch between illustrated ingredients and step-by-step directions. Open All steps or Notes to see more.
- Groceries: see which recipes you are shopping for, browse items by aisle, and add an item using the floating glass field above the tabs.
- Check and uncheck items, swipe to delete from any aisle or the basket, and confirm the correct item changes.
- Check light/dark appearance, large text, and the add-item field with the keyboard open.

- Save a recipe using only a photo and title.
- Import a recipe from a website with structured recipe information.
- Browse recipes, open recipe details, and add ingredients to the grocery list.
- Check and uncheck groceries.
- Close and reopen the app to check that recipes and groceries persist, including offline.

Household invitations and AI-assisted recipe cleanup are still in development.
