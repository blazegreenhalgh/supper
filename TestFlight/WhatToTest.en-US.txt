Supper: shared cookbook and household update

• Recipe detail: immersive full-width photo, one compact reaction menu, smaller ingredient icons and inline amount capsules.
• Edit recipes, photos, source, notes, tags, base servings, ingredient groups/categories, steps and multiple collections. Cancel keeps the original; failed saves keep your draft.
• Change cooking servings without changing the original recipe. Add to Groceries previews the scaled amounts.
• Review and recover ingredient groups from the original source. Switch between source groups and shopping categories.
• Groceries combine compatible weights, volumes and counts, retain contributing recipes, and keep checked/removed items removed during retries.
• Pick something respects search, tags, duration, collections and reactions. Create collections and arrange their homepage sections.
• On-device recipe text/screenshot assistance, tag suggestions and natural-language search; manual entry and text recognition remain available without Apple Intelligence.
• Household settings: set your name, invite/manage sharing, accept an invitation, and switch libraries without deleting your existing library.

Please check normal back, swipe-back and cancelled swipe-back with an active search; Dynamic Type and Reduce Transparency; editing and grocery sync between both phones. CloudKit production schema must include the new fields, collection/member types and sharing record type before this build is distributed to existing households.
